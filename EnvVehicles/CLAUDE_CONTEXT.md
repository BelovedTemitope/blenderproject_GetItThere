### The user's rules
- **Topology and style:** Synty-style low poly, "use less to speak more". Clean, quad edge modelling, edge flow where needed, no wandering loops, no n-gons.
- **Polygon count:** start from a ballpark of 500–800 polys per vehicle, then use as few as possible while keeping the silhouette, form and logic.
  - The ballpark is not a hard limit. More geometry is fine where it is needed to match the concept.
- **Fidelity is the priority:** "looks exactly like the concept", capture each vehicle's essence.
  - Iterate until the overlays are near perfect in shape, form **and landmarks**: windows, stripes, lamps, wheels, pillars.
  - Use real-life references for unseen views.
- **Scale:** ignore the minibus's 18×8×9 spec here. The concept drives the proportions; 1 unit = 1 stud.
- **Deliverables:**
  - Renders, including a **wireframe pass**.
  - The downloadable .blend sent in chat.
  - Everything pushed to GitHub.
- **Base decisions** carry over from the minibus work (v05 was the best detailed work): functional, logical parts; parts fit together; layered details kept as separate objects.
- **User feedback so far:**
  - "we don't need perfection here, a decent one, later this week we upgrade it". Then: iterate to near-perfect overlays.
  - **"I don't like your D and B at all"**, which triggered the rebuilds below.

## Files

- **`EnvVehicles/reference/Env_Vehicles_People_Concept.webp`:** the concept sheet (vehicles A–E plus people F–L; only A–D are in scope).
- **`EnvVehicles/GIT_Env_Vehicles_ABCD_v01.blend`:** last saved build (older than the code; rebuild it).
  - Collections are `GIT_Env_Vehicles/<GIT_Env_Danfo_A | GIT_Env_Danfo_B_Wrecked | GIT_Env_Taxi_C | GIT_Env_Keke_D>`.
  - Each vehicle is a Body plus wheels with their origin at the hub, plus layered details (A roof rack and cargo, C roof sign, D canopy), all parented to the Body.
  - B uses `textures/T_Env_DanfoB_Paint.png`, packed into the file. B's body and D's canopy are single-sided shells, so set DoubleSided in Roblox.
- **`tools/env_vehicles/`:** the procedural build. Every model is generated by these scripts.
  - `ev_lib.py`: `grid_box` (quad lattice box with explicit cut lines; a `deform` callback shapes it, and `face_mat` assigns materials or leaves openings), insets, wheel wells, wheels, `uv_tiles`, `orient_outward`.
  - `ev_danfo.py` (A and B; `configure('high' | 'standard')`), `ev_rust.py` (B paint atlas), `ev_taxi.py` (C), `ev_keke.py` (D).
  - `ev_build.py -- <out.blend> [A B C D]` builds everything.
  - Concept matching:
    - `ev_concept_mask.py <concept> <dir>` makes per-vehicle silhouette masks.
    - `ev_dump_tris.py -- <blend> <dir>` dumps triangles plus colour classes.
    - `ev_fitcam.py` fits a silhouette camera; `ev_fitland.py` refines it on landmarks (silhouette plus dark and glass regions).
    - `ev_fitprop.py` fits camera plus length/height scale, to find proportion errors.
  - Checks:
    - `ev_render.py -- <blend> <prefix> <key> [views] [--albedo]` renders review views; `concept` is the fitted concept camera, read from `concept_cams.json` or `$EV_CAMS`.
    - `ev_landmarks.py` gives per-colour-region IoU.
    - `ev_blend.py` gives the zoomed 50/50 blend plus magenta model edges, the main visual check.
  - `dev/iterate.sh <work_dir> [keys]` runs build → render → landmark scores → blend images. `dev/sheet.py` builds contact sheets.
- **Fitted cameras:** `tools/env_vehicles/concept_cams.json`. The concept is AI-drawn and its perspective is not consistent across vehicles, so each vehicle has its own camera.

## Environment

- **Headless Blender:**
  - The pip `bpy==5.2.2` module, in a uv venv at `/opt/bpyenv` (Python 3.13, plus numpy, pillow, scipy).
  - Recreate it with `uv venv /opt/bpyenv --python 3.13 && uv pip install --python /opt/bpyenv/bin/python bpy==5.2.2 pillow scipy`.
  - Import `bpy` before `bmesh`, and never name a script after a stdlib module.
- **Network:** Wikimedia rate-limits this cloud IP, and bajajauto.com didn't resolve. The real-reference dimensions used are:
  - T3: 4.57 × 1.85 × 1.93 m, wheelbase 2.46 m.
  - Bajaj RE: 2.64 × 1.30 × 1.70 m, wheelbase 2.0 m.
  - Peugeot 504: 4.49 × 1.69 × 1.46 m, wheelbase 2.74 m.
  - The concept is chunkier than all of these, so the overlay governs.

## Coordinates

- Front is −Y, +X is the vehicle's left, Z is up.
- Each vehicle's Body origin is at ground centre, at its lineup location in the file.
